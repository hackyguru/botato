The blob, specified

One family, drawn to a written rule rather than by hand. Every outline here is generated — a radius that varies with angle, smoothed into one continuous curve and scaled so all twelve carry the same optical mass. That is what makes a set look designed rather than collected: no kinks where two curves were spliced, no character noticeably heavier than the one beside it, and eyes in the same place on every body.

The paths at the bottom are the real output — they can go straight into the app.

The rule

every drawing obeys these

Outline
A radius that varies with angle, r(θ) = 1 + Σ harmonics + Σ lobes, sampled at 96 points and closed with a Catmull-Rom spline. Curvature is continuous everywhere, so there is no seam to find.
Mass
Every body is scaled to the same area, not the same width. A pointed shape is wider at the base than a round one so the two weigh the same in a column.
Eyes
Diameter 0.15 of the body's width, centres 0.26 apart, on a line 0.06 below the optical centre. Never scaled per character.
Gaze
Both pupils shift up to ±0.02 together — the only per-character licence, and the whole of what makes one look attentive and another dreamy.
Light
One source, upper left. A soft highlight at 32% 22%, a contact shadow along the lower edge. No outline, ever, at any size.
Silhouette
Differences are large and few: a point, a pair of ears, a notch. Nothing that needs more than four pixels to be seen.

Asked for, in words

What a bot would get back if it asked. Two layers reach it: the silhouette, which is parameters — a lobe is an ear, a narrow one is a horn, a negative one is a bite — and props, drawn on top against the eye line and the body box rather than against any one shape, so the same glasses land correctly on a drop, a cat and a cloud.

This is a better surface to prompt than what botcage has now. A model is poor at bezier paths and good at "a tall bump at the top" — and every value it can choose still obeys the spec: the generator normalises the mass, keeps the curve smooth and puts the eyes where the rule says. You cannot prompt your way to an off-model character.
