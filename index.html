Welcome to botcage

            

              Bots live on this machine. Each one keeps its own memory and workspace, and can be
              given a private Linux desktop to work on.
            

            
            
A few more things to settle — it takes a minute.

          


          

            
What answers your bots

            

              botcage ships no model of its own. Pick one way to start — any bot can be changed
              later, and different bots can use different ones.
            


            

              

              

              
            


            

              
              Checking…
            

            

            

              Runs Anthropic's own installer, https://claude.ai/install.sh, as you —
              no admin password, everything under your home folder.
            

          


          

            
A computer for your bots

            

              Optional. A bot given its own computer gets a sandboxed Linux desktop with a browser
              and a terminal, which you can watch it use. Everything else works without this.
            

            

              
              Checking…
            

            

          


          

            
Talking to your bots

            

              Optional. You can call a bot and talk to it — it listens, thinks and answers out
              loud, all on this machine. That needs a speech model and a set of voices; they can
              come now, or the first time you make a call.
